# Platform marks

Bundled SVG platform logos were obtained from `https://logos.composio.dev/api/<toolkit-slug>` on September 9, 2026. Each filename identifies its toolkit slug. Logos identify third-party services in the connection catalog; all marks belong to their respective owners and do not imply endorsement or affiliation. Additional catalog logos are fetched by Electron from the same fixed HTTPS service and rendered as images, with a text fallback.
