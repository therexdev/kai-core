Pocket voice credits

KAI uses Azelma Pocket by default on supported computers. Its pinned model and presets download automatically when KAI opens; other voices remain available. No voice reference recording or conversation is uploaded for speech synthesis.

  • Pocket TTS by Kyutai: MIT. English model converted by KevinAHM, CC BY 4.0; repackaged and quantized by the sherpa-onnx team. Sherpa runtime: Apache 2.0.
  • Kyutai voice presets: Alba uses Alba MacKenna’s casual recording (CC BY 4.0). Azelma uses VCTK p303_023_enhanced (CC BY 4.0). Marius (Selfie) and Javert (Butter) use donated recordings released under CC0.
  • KAI applies optional pitch and texture effects to generated audio. These effects and generated speech are adaptations; the original speakers do not endorse KAI. No non-commercial-only test recordings are included.

The exact pinned model’s license is downloaded with its files. KAI uses the January 2026 English model; it does not add Korean speech support.
